# Decisions

Why the site looks and reads the way it does. Newest first within each topic; change a line here when a decision changes.

## Content

- **Languages:** English is the default, German lives under `/de/`. Italian is out until there is a native localization – a translation is not enough.
- **Brand elements stay English** in every language: claim, tagline, motto, the four Ps, the slogans and the tree labels (Scope, Team, Result – as in the #JobAlert posts).
- **Localized, not translated:** each language follows its own conventions – the dictionary entry in Merriam-Webster style (EN) and Duden style (DE), Trient instead of Trento in German, KUR in German and RoAS in English.
- **Current employer:** no internal cost figures, absolute values rounded ("more than doubled", "more than half a million").
- **No first-person monologue.** Telegram style, facts instead of adjectives.
- **Four coined elements, no more:** the claim, the motto, *marketinkerer* and P⁴. Ten were too many (feedback, 29.10.2026); cut were "Everything human:e.", the footer line "Admiring complexity. Loving simplicity.", "Stop starting. Start finishing." and the Approach slogans "Facts, not fluff." · "Built, not briefed." · "Prompted, not pasted." – their sentences stay. AI is named once as a principle, in Approach, never as a label on single projects.
- **No notes behind an *i*:** the last one, "Processes fit people, not the other way round." after People, went with its button, its CSS and its script (04.11.2026). What the page says stands in the text.
- **Profile text:** "interdisciplinary marketing and design teams"; the certificates without the school (Liora and the Sorbonne stand in Experience and Education); the working languages German, English, Italian (feedback, 02.11.2026).
- **P⁴:** four fields without an intro line ("P as in Peter" dropped), each text three lines long on a wide screen and on a phone (11.11.2026): Performance "optimized for RoAS and cost per lead" (DE: "gesteuert über KUR und Kosten je Lead") instead of "cost per inquiry, quote and order"; Pipelines without "on Google Cloud"; Pixels without "templates" and without tool names – Python already stands under Pipelines, and Inkscape and Canva went with it. Performance without SEO (it stays in the roles where it was done), "optimized for" instead of the rarer "steered by". Pipelines without "Consent Mode v2" – too specific. Data Studio keeps its name: Google renamed it Looker Studio in 2022 and back to Data Studio in April 2026 (03.11.2026). Pixels names what is made – corporate identity, websites, employer branding, mockups, vector graphics, videos. People: teams of up to nine – the size at SportSpar, the largest, as in the profile – "including career changers from retail and fine arts" (DE: "darunter Quereinsteiger aus Einzelhandel und bildender Kunst") – where they came from shows what the team was built on; the former employers stay unnamed (03.11.2026). "Trained in performance marketing and data analysis" was dropped, because nobody came trained (02.11.2026).
- **Order:** newest first everywhere – the timeline, the steps inside an entry, the ledger, Education, Projects. One direction, so nothing reads as reversed.
- **Ledger:** three results, newest first, each opening with what was measured – Leads, RoAS, Media budget (DE: Leads, KUR, Budget) – then one line of context of about the same length, then the company on its own line: every result two lines under its figure, stacked at every width. The name links to the role under Experience, marked with a superscript ↓ in the accent colour – like the footnote numbers, it says "more on this page" (↗ would mean "leaves the site"). "17 shops in 12 languages" was dropped as fluff (02.11.2026), and with it the second Posterlounge row.
- **Roles:** the same tree labels wherever the facts exist – Team, Scope, Result; a role shows only what is known rather than filler. Every role shows its length under the dates, in full words, counted like LinkedIn (first and last month included); the running one is recounted on every visit. Posterlounge keeps "Head of Marketing" as its heading – easier to read than the whole path; its four titles open behind "Three promotions", newest first, each with its dates and length from the contracts – real titles are what people search for. The two Liora courses stay in the timeline, marked as trainings: full-time from 02 to 07/2025, they show what that stretch was without reading as jobs. Their school reads "Liora (formerly DataScientest)" – the name on the certificates and the one people search for. Marketing automation is named where it was done (Brandible, Rapidobject). 2010–2014 stays one entry in the timeline (with exact months); its five positions open behind "All five positions", listed like the promotions – title, company, dates and length.
- **Approach:** three short sentences – documented figures, briefs from someone who knows the systems, AI with every result checked. The aphorisms around them ("Growth only counts when efficiency keeps pace", "And less necessary.") read as filler and went (02.11.2026).
- **Wordplay:** only Pete's own or in his patterns. Tried and dropped: "einSTIEG" as a heading, "See also: root access", "The other four Ps".
- **Cloud:** Google Cloud is a tool; Azure appears only as the platform of single projects. IT administration (Intune, GPO) is not on the site.
- **Certificates:** the profile names them without a year (the year is in Education, where it belongs). Two separate bootcamps (Data Scientist 02–05/2025, MLOps Engineer 05–07/2025), each with a footnote linking the verification page from the diploma's QR code and the course page.
- **More proof:** the IHK certificate (2023, the IHK as issuer, like the other certificates) and the edX course "Introduction to Artificial Intelligence" by Microsoft (2018) carry footnotes to their verification pages too. The capstones link their Streamlit demos next to GitHub. Every company in Experience links its website where one exists and is verified – the roles and four of the five early positions: Inner Valor links innervalor.com, SBS its Italian site it.sbsbusiness.eu (11.11.2026); Proactive Europe stays unlinked (04.11.2026). Every new link target is named in the privacy statement.
- **Languages:** one per line – easier to scan than a row with dots.
- **Contact:** four rows, laid out like Education – Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Based in (Wohnort) – with the addresses as link texts, so they can be read off a printout and typed. The greeting "Hallo · Hi · Ciao" in large type and the places before Leipzig were dropped (feedback, 30.10.2026); the greeting stays in the source comment. Every value is a link with a raised mark in the ↗'s place – an envelope for the mail program, ↗ for the two profiles, a pin for the map (03.11.2026). The map is OpenStreetMap with a pin on Coppiplatz in Gohlis; Google Maps would show visitors in the EU a consent page first.
- **Description** (search snippet, link previews): the coined word and the place, the two fields of the title and the banner – lead generation stays in the roles where it was done (Brandible, E+SERVICE+CHECK) –, then three facts: four leadership roles, up to €240k media budget a month, the certificate. A figure instead of "proven track record": every profile claims that, the ledger proves this. 150 characters, about what Google shows uncut. "Self-built tools" went; the definition of *marketinkerer* says it on the page (03.11.2026).
- **Not on the site:** recommendations as quotes, photo (pending).

## Design

- **Typeface:** Atkinson Hyperlegible, self-hosted, no font service.
- **Colour:** bordeaux as the only accent (prompt, focus, footnotes, hover). Light and dark themes with the same structure.
- **Hero:** same order as the LinkedIn banner – name, tagline, claim, prompt `>_ sudo dream plan ship`.
- **Logo:** the lockup `>_ P/S` ("Ein Takt", decided 25.10.2026; v2 29.10.2026: `>_` raised 8.63 units, so the `_` ends exactly on the P's foot instead of missing it by a hair) in the header, 28 px tall, inline so it follows light and dark. Source and generator in `src/lockup/`. The monogram alone (final 23.10.2026, `src/monogram/`) is the favicon set (on a bordeaux disc since 30.10.2026), the mark in the link preview (`og-image.png`), the avatar and the footer of printed documents.
- **Docking:** while scrolling, PETER STIEG and `>_` travel into the header as the wordmark `>_ PETER STIEG`, so the name is visible once. Scrolling on (180 px), the wordmark condenses into the lockup – the closing step of `src/lockup/animation/ps-morph.html`, scroll-driven and reversible. Reduced motion: the lockup appears instantly.
- **Minimap:** on wide screens, the page drawn as code lines like the banner; click or drag to jump. The four chapter headings are drawn in the accent, like their numbers on the page, so the outline shows at a glance (30.10.2026).
- **Jump control:** once the name has docked, a pill bottom right (under the minimap on wide screens): up, the chapter number, down. Up and down go to the start of the section above and below, the number opens a list of all sections. It replaced the round back-to-top button. Without JavaScript: up to the top and the list.
- **A heading always in view:** on wide screens the section heading sticks in its column; on phones it sticks under the header as one bar with it. The menu (wide screens) and the jump control's number (all screens) name the chapter.
- **Heading sizes on phones** (11.11.2026): the section heading 26 px, the headword 24 px, the P⁴ headings 22 px. Stacked above its content, the section heading has to outrank what follows it; at 20 px it was smaller than both the headword (27 px and more) and the P⁴ headings. Wide screens keep 20 px: the heading's own column sets it apart, and at 26 px "02 Experience" wraps there.
- **Theme switch** (11.11.2026): a pressed button is a stored pick; pressed again, it lets go, the entry is deleted and the device decides again – a way back without clearing site data (closes issue #10). With no pick, neither button is pressed: the buttons show a choice, not what the system shows.
- **Four chapters, like the parts of a slide deck:** 01 Profile (with P⁴), 02 Experience (with Education), 03 Projects (with Approach), 04 Contact (with Sources). The number stands in front of the chapter's heading, in the accent colour and regular weight; the sections inside a chapter carry the same number invisibly, so all titles line up like an outline. Education moved up behind Experience for this.
- **Menu numbers:** the four menu links are the four chapters, numbered like their headings – no gaps. Below 56rem the numbers drop so the header fits.
- **You are here:** while scrolling, the menu link of the chapter being read looks like its heading – number in the accent colour, label in ink and bold – also in the sections that belong to it; at the very bottom, Contact. The bold width is reserved, so the menu never shifts.
- **Torch** (03.11.2026): the page's code under a light that follows the pointer – root access, shown. Typing `sudo` switches it on; typed again, it goes one level deeper – the HTML, then the lines of `build.py` that wrote it – and the third time ends it; so does Esc. `?` lists the keys, as on GitHub and in terminal tools like htop and lazygit – in a card laid out like Instagram's: title and close button, what each shortcut does on the left, the key as a key cap on the right (04.11.2026). Keyboard only: a `</>` button next to the theme switch and a click on the cursor were built and dropped to keep the interface clean. Source comment and console greeting name both keys. Computers only (mouse or trackpad): a light that follows the pointer needs a pointer. No *i* after "root access". On every page since 11.11.2026: the start pages, the legal pages and the 404s.
- **Cursor:** blinks three times, then stays solid – plain, without a `?` in the third blink (tried and dropped, 03.11.2026). 3.3 seconds, under WCAG 2.2.2's 5; no blinking for those who asked for less motion.
- **Links that leave the site** open in a new tab, so the page stays open behind them. A muted ↗ marks them, in accent on hover – quieter than the accent ↓, so twenty-two of them do not clutter the page; screen readers hear "opens in a new tab". Mail, menu, language switch and legal pages stay in the same tab.
- **Arrows and tree lines are drawn**, because the typeface has no → glyph and box-drawing characters depend on the font.

## Tech

- **No framework, no build tools:** one Python script, plain CSS, one small script file.
- **Favicons:** the PS in paper on a bordeaux disc (v2, 30.10.2026), one version for light and dark. The bare bordeaux mark before it nearly vanished on dark tab strips (1.2 : 1); the disc brings its own ground. The pages link only `favicon.ico` (16 and 32 px drawn by hand, 48 px) – browsers prefer an SVG when one is linked, and at 16 px the vector comes out soft; `favicon.svg` stays on the server, unlinked. `apple-touch-icon.png` and the manifest's 192 and 512 px icons are full-bleed bordeaux with the PS inside the maskable safe zone. `.htaccess` gives the manifest its MIME type.
- **Plain text that reads right:** search engines and parsers read the page as text, so what CSS draws must not carry meaning alone – arrows keep the word "to" as hidden text, the tree labels carry their colon in the HTML. Checked by extracting the text.
- **Findability:** Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and links from LinkedIn and GitHub – tracked in issue #19.
- **Torch data:** the build writes `assets/torch.json` – for each kind of element, the line of `build.py` that writes it, found by a snippet, so the numbers stay right after every edit. The legal pages are mapped by position instead (11.11.2026): their texts are written one element per line, so the n-th element of a page's body is the n-th line of its string – no rule per element, and a new paragraph needs none. `site.js` fetches it the first time the torch is switched on; the strings for the button and the keys card ride in one data attribute, so the page's text stays free of them. The monospace face (Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono) is registered but only downloaded when the torch or the card shows.
- **Privacy:** no cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests. The theme choice is stored in the visitor's browser (localStorage) only after a click, and deleted by a second click on the same button – both privacy statements say so (11.11.2026).
- **Legal pages in English** (11.11.2026): `legal-notice.html` and `privacy.html` translate the Impressum and the Datenschutzerklärung. The German text is binding; each translation says so in its second line and links the original. Own file names, all four in the site root – the German pages keep their addresses. Each pair is linked by the language switch and by hreflang; the footer links the pages in the page's language.
- **Sitemap:** generated by the build; it lists pages, not content, so it only needs a rebuild when pages are added or removed. `lastmod` is the build date.
- **404:** one per language (11.11.2026): `404.html` in English with a German line, `de/404.html` in German (`{KeyWord:Seite} nicht gefunden.`) with an English one, each linking both start pages. `de/.htaccess` serves the German one for every missing address under `/de/`. `{KeyWord:Page} not found.` – Google Ads Dynamic Keyword Insertion, for those who know. `.htaccess` activates it on Apache hosting.
- **403 shows the 404 page** (11.11.2026): a forbidden address gets the site's own page instead of Apache's; the status stays 403.
- **One address** (11.11.2026): `www.` and `http://` forward with a 301 to `https://peterstieg.com` – the address every canonical tag already names. The check is the port, as IONOS recommends.
- **Caching and compression** (11.11.2026): `site.css` and `site.js` carry `?v=` plus the first 8 hex digits of their SHA-256, written by the build. A changed file gets a new address, so browsers keep both, and the fonts, for a year (`immutable`); pages and `torch.json` are checked on every visit (`no-cache` – unchanged, a short 304). Text goes out compressed (mod_deflate): start page 32 → 8 KB, `site.js` 34 → 12 KB, `site.css` 26 → 7 KB, measured on a local Apache.
- **Deploy:** manual upload of `site/` to IONOS. Auto-deploy via GitHub Actions is an open option.
- **peterstieg.github.io forwards to peterstieg.com** instead of mirroring the site: one address for search engines, and the privacy statement (host: IONOS) stays true.
- **Forward page:** a two-second terminal scene instead of "Moved to …" – `mv -v peterstieg.github.io peterstieg.com`, the real `mv -v` output, `cd peterstieg.com` (with the path for deep links), then on. Click or Enter skips, Esc or Ctrl+C stops (`^C`). Reduced motion: no typing, forward after 1.2 s; no JavaScript: plain text, refresh after 3 s. Typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, self-hosted in `forward/fonts/` – no third-party request.
