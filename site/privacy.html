In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from third parties. Fonts, scripts and graphics are stored on the same server as the page itself. Only if you switch between the light and dark design yourself does your browser remember that choice (see below).

Hosting and server log files

When you visit the website, the hosting provider automatically processes data your browser transmits: IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, referrer URL, browser and operating system. This is technically necessary to deliver the page and keep it secure. The legal basis is legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Hosting provider: IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with it. How long the log files are kept is set by the provider.

Light and dark design

Unless you choose otherwise, the website follows your device's setting. If you choose the design yourself with the switch at the top, your browser stores this choice locally (Local Storage) so it is kept for your next visit. The entry stays on your device and is not sent to the server. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. You can delete the entry at any time: with a second click on the chosen design, or in your browser's settings.

Contact by email

If you write an email, the details you provide are processed to answer your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Links to other platforms

The site links to LinkedIn, GitHub, a map on OpenStreetMap, the course pages of Liora, the verification pages of DataScientest, thekey.career and edX, the demos of the capstone projects on Streamlit, and the websites of former and current employers. Only when you follow one of these links do the respective providers process data, under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible where you live.
